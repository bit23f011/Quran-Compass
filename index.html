Sources & conventions

The catalogue follows the common 6,236-ayah numbering. Revelation order and Meccan/Medinan classifications are conventional and may differ between scholarly sources. Chapter overviews and reflection prompts are study introductions, not tafsir. Detailed tafsir references open on Quran.com.
